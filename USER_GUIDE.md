# Fleex User Guide

## Overview
Fleex is a creator-first platform where you forge your digital identity through customizable micro-apps called **Forges**. Build your presence, follow creators, discover new content, and interact with the community.

---

## Navigation & Core Features

### 1. **Navigation Bar** (Top of every page)
The sticky navigation bar at the top gives you quick access to:
- **Fleex Logo** - Go to dashboard (if logged in) or home page
- **Search Bar** - Find creators by name or username
- **Spark** - Discover new Forges (personalized feed)
- **My Forges** - Manage your Forges in the dashboard
- **Profile** - Visit your public profile
- **Logout** - Sign out of Fleex

---

## Main Sections

### 2. **Home Page** (`/`)
- Landing page explaining Fleex
- Sign up or login to get started
- Call-to-actions for creating Forges and discovering creators

### 3. **Dashboard** (`/dashboard`)
Your personal creation hub where you can:
- **View all your Forges** - See all Forges you've created
- **Create new Forge** - Click "Create Forge" to make a new one
- **Edit Forges** - Click on any Forge to edit its settings and content
- **Publish/Unpublish** - Toggle whether Forges are visible to others
- **Delete Forges** - Remove Forges you no longer want

#### Creating a Forge:
1. Go to Dashboard → "Create Forge"
2. Choose a **template type**:
   - **Portfolio** - Showcase your work and skills
   - **Blog** - Write and share articles
   - **Gallery** - Display images and photos
   - **Shop** - Sell products or services
   - **Donation** - Accept donations from supporters
   - **Game** - Create interactive experiences
   - **Custom** - Code your own unique Forge
3. Name your Forge and add a description
4. Configure the template settings
5. **Publish** to make it visible on your profile

---

### 4. **Your Profile** (`/profile/[username]`)
Your public identity page featuring:

#### **Face** - What everyone sees:
- **Cover Photo** - Eye-catching header image
- **Avatar** - Your profile picture
- **Display Name** - How others see you
- **Bio** - Short description about you
- **Your Forges** - Showcase your creations in a drag-and-drop canvas

#### **Editing Your Profile:**
- Click **Edit Profile** button (only visible on your own profile)
- Upload/change your **avatar photo**
- Upload/change your **cover photo**
- Edit your **display name** and **bio**
- Drag and drop Forges on your Face to customize their layout
- Save when done

#### **Profile Features:**
- **Follow Button** - Other users can follow you to get your updates
- **Followers/Following** - See who follows you (coming soon)
- **Forges Display** - Your published Forges appear here

---

### 5. **Spark Feed** (`/spark`)
Your personalized discovery engine:

#### **How it Works:**
The Spark feed shows you Forges recommended based on:
- **Allies (+10 points)** - Forges from creators you follow get prioritized
- **Interest Matching (+5 points)** - Templates you interact with get more recommendations
- **Recency Bonus** - New Forges (last 24h: +5 pts, last 7d: +3 pts) appear first
- **Your Interactions** - Likes, views, and comments influence future recommendations

#### **Using Spark:**
1. Go to **Spark** in the top navigation
2. Scroll through recommended Forges
3. **Click** on a Forge to view it in detail
4. **Follow** the creator if you like their work
5. **Like, Comment, or Share** Forges to influence your feed

---

### 6. **Search** (`/search`)
Find creators and profiles:

#### **How to Search:**
1. Click the **Search icon** in navigation (mobile) or use the **search bar** (desktop)
2. Type a creator's **username** or **display name**
3. Press Enter or click Search
4. Results show all matching creators
5. Click on a result to visit their profile

#### **What You Can Find:**
- Usernames and display names
- User bio/description
- Avatar pictures
- Direct links to their profiles

## Understanding the Spark Algorithm

### Feed Scoring System:
```
Forge Score = Base Score + Bonuses

Base Score = Template Match (if you've interacted with similar templates)

Bonuses:
+ 10 points = From someone you follow (Ally)
+  5 points = Template type you've liked before
+  5 points = Created in last 24 hours
+  3 points = Created in last 7 days
+  1 point  = Views from other users
```

### Why Your Feed Changes:
- **More interactions** = More personalized recommendations
- **Following creators** = Their Forges rank higher
- **Pattern detection** = System learns your preferences

## Troubleshooting

**Profile not found?**
- Make sure you've signed up and completed setup
- Check your username spelling
- Ensure you're logged in

**Forges not showing?**
- Make sure they're published (not draft)
- Check your Spark feed - they may be lower down
- Try refreshing the page

**Photo upload failing?**
- Check file size (max 5MB recommended)
- Try a different image format (JPG, PNG)
- Clear browser cache and try again
